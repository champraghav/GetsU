Common questions

How do I order and pay?

Add products to your cart, review it on the cart page, then continue to the delivery and payment page. There you enter your delivery details and choose how to pay — online, by UPI, or by sending us the order and paying once we confirm. You never need an account.

Can I cancel a payment?

Yes. Until a payment goes through, every payment screen has a Cancel payment button. It cancels the order, takes no money, and leaves your cart exactly as it was so you can change it and try again. Once you have paid, message us your order ID and we'll sort it out.

What does "clean label" actually mean?

Every ingredient on the pack is a real food you can recognise. No artificial preservatives, no hidden fillers, no proprietary blends that hide how little of something is in there. Tap any product to see its full ingredient list before you buy.

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch within 1–2 business days and delivery usually takes 3–6 business days anywhere in India. Shipping is free with no minimum order. You'll get tracking details on the phone number you give at checkout.

I have a food allergy — is this safe for me?

Every product lists its allergens in full. The Protein Bar contains almonds, cashews and peanuts; the High-Protein Oats contain milk, almonds and oats; the Pancake Premix contains oats. Everything is packed in a facility that also handles milk and nuts, so we can't guarantee against trace cross-contact. Always check the physical pack before eating.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we'll replace it or refund you in full, at no cost to you. Because these are food products we can't accept returns on opened packs otherwise.
